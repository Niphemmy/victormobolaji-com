The Revenue Roadmap Checklist

The checks every founder should run before spending another naira on growth. Work down the list. Any box you cannot tick is where your next naira is leaking.

Print this, or save it as a PDF (use your browser's print dialog), and run it against your business this week.

01The one-sentence offer

A stranger can tell, in one sentence, what they get, what it costs, and why it is worth far more than the price.

02Value stacked past 10x

The value you deliver is stacked to at least ten times the price, with a real guarantee, not a hollow one.

03A funnel, not just a page

There is a clear path from attention to a booked call or sale, with a next step at every stage.

04The customer's real pain, in their words

You can write the customer's three-in-the-morning thought and the exact phrase they type into Google.

05Pricing tied to value, not cost

Your price reflects the outcome the customer gets, not the hours you spent or what a competitor charges.

06Unit economics that reconcile

You know what a customer costs to acquire and what they are worth over their life, in real numbers.

07Cash flow you can see

You can say what is coming in and going out over the next ninety days without guessing.

08A KPI dashboard

A handful of numbers, in one place, that tell you whether the business is winning this week.

09A value ladder with no dead ends

Every offer leads to a bigger one. Nothing you sell is a final stop.

10SOPs for what repeats

The work that happens every week is written down, so results do not depend on one person's memory.

Persuasion

A website is not a funnel. It is a brochure that loads.

Victor Mobolaji · 3 min read

A funnel is a screenplay where the customer is the protagonist. A website, on its own, is set dressing. Beautiful, and inert.

The difference is direction. A brochure presents. A funnel moves. Every section has a job: catch attention, name the false belief the reader holds, break it, prove the claim, and offer one clear next step. Nothing is decorative. Everything points to the close.

Why this matters for your money

When you run ads or post content into a brochure, the visitor admires it and leaves. When you run the same traffic into a funnel, the visitor is carried, step by step, to a decision. Same traffic, completely different result. The machine is the variable, not the volume.

What to build instead

  • A hook that earns the next five seconds.
  • A story that makes the reader feel the problem.
  • An offer engineered so the yes is the logical choice.
  • One next step, repeated, never buried.

Build the machine, then send the traffic. Not the other way around.

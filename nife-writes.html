Nife Writes

Nife Writes is the pen name of Victor Mobolaji, and the writing practice he runs through his company, Nife Writing Consult, founded in 2022. Academic, professional, and business writing that reads human and lands like an expert built it.

Three lines

From a CV to a doctoral chapter to a business plan.

Professional & career writing

Resumes and CVs, LinkedIn revamps, cover letters, statements of purpose, personal statements, letters of interest, motivation letters, academic CVs. The client is the product, and the writing has to make a stranger choose them.

Academic & research writing

Dissertations, theses, assessments, assignments, case studies, and research across disciplines. The high-stakes end, where the margin for error is zero. I write to that pressure.

Business writing

Business plans, proposals, consumer and market research, financial research. This is where the writer became the strategist, no longer just putting thoughts on paper but building the thinking.

The standard

Human-sounding, rubric-aligned, citation-correct, on deadline.

The work reads like the client wrote it, clean, with none of the fingerprints of machine-made text. No em dashes, no filler, no vocabulary that exists only to impress. If there is a rubric, the work is built around it, section by section, hitting the target length without padding and without cutting a corner that costs a grade.

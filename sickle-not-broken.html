UNBENT. UNYIELDING. UNBROKEN.

The refusal

They tried to reduce me to one word.

One diagnosis. One sheet of paper. One random test, used to sum up my whole life. I refused. And if you are a warrior, this book is your refusal too.

Sickle Not Broken is not a medical guide. It is not a motivational speech. It is a conversation between two warriors. One who has fought this war for over three decades, and one who is still learning how to survive the next battle without losing their mind, their hope, or their sense of who they are beyond the diagnosis.

This book does not flinch. It meets you in the pain, sits with you in the dark for as long as you need, and then, when you are ready, it puts fire on your bones.
What is inside

Built like a war manual, because that is exactly what your life is.

Part One

The Battlefield

What a crisis really feels like. The pain no medication can touch. The names the world hands us. The hospitals that were supposed to save us. Growing up in a body that fights itself.

Part Two

The Wounds

The friends we have buried. The things we have never said out loud. The family we were given. The mind as the real battleground. And faith, when the prayers feel like they hit the ceiling and fall back down.

Part Three

The Arsenal

Reclaim your identity on your own terms. Understand your body like a scientist. Build yourself into someone the world cannot ignore. Turn your health into a weapon, not a weakness. Love without apology.

Part Four

The Advance

Career, money, family, and community for a warrior whose body demands rest at unpredictable moments. And a final letter, from me to you.

Who it is for

For more than warriors.

It is for every warrior who has ever been called a sickler. And it is for the people standing beside them.

  • The families, partners, and friends who love a warrior and have watched the world reduce them to a label.
  • The caregivers who carry this fight alongside us, often with no thanks and no rest.
  • The teachers, employers, and health workers who want to do better by the warriors in their care.
  • Anyone ready to unlearn the misconceptions and finally see the human being the diagnosis tries to hide.

The author

I am still here. Still building.

I am Victor Oluwanifemi Mobolaji. I write as Nife_Writes, and I have lived with sickle cell my entire life. I came from nothing and built everything with my own hands, one of the largest writing practices in Nigeria, and now a growing business consulting firm. I have seen death more times than I can count. I am still here.

This book is dedicated to the warriors I lost to this disease, to Faleelat, Precious, and Seun. Their memory is the reason these pages exist.

The method

S.T.A.R.

Everything Northern Star produces has to pass one test before it leaves my hands. Open each one.

"Improve your marketing" is not a recommendation. "Run an Instagram campaign targeting professionals aged twenty five to thirty four, five hundred thousand naira a month, three percent click-through by the second quarter" is.
Every recommendation is rooted in data, market insight, or a proven framework. No platitudes. If I tell a client to do something, I can show the analysis underneath it.
Nothing is a template with the name swapped out. A street food vendor and a SaaS founder get completely different treatment. If a sentence in one client's document could sit unchanged in another's, it gets rewritten.
Every recommendation says who does it, what they do, when, and how success gets measured. A vague instruction is not a plan.
Every document answers one question without flinching: what is the financial impact of doing this? "Increase revenue" is banned. "Reach sixty five million naira within twelve months, one hundred and sixty seven percent growth" is the standard.
